FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there funded PhD positions available?
PLACEHOLDER: Yes/No and details about funding. Typically PhD students are supported through research assistantships (RA), teaching assistantships (TA), or fellowships. Please check the Apply page and contact Dr. Zhou directly for current openings.
What backgrounds are you looking for in PhD applicants?
PLACEHOLDER: We welcome students from diverse backgrounds including geography, GIScience, computer science, data science, environmental science, civil engineering, and related fields. A strong interest in geospatial AI and natural hazards is more important than a specific background.
When should I apply to the PhD program?
PLACEHOLDER: The UTK Geography PhD program has deadlines of approximately [MONTH] for fall admission. We recommend reaching out to Dr. Zhou several months before the application deadline to discuss your interests and ensure fit. See the Apply page for details.
Can I do a research internship or visiting scholar position?
PLACEHOLDER: We occasionally host visiting scholars and research interns depending on project needs and funding. Please contact Dr. Zhou with your CV, a description of your research interests, and proposed timeline.
I am not in Knoxville — can I collaborate remotely?
PLACEHOLDER: Remote collaboration is possible for external collaborators and visiting researchers. However, PhD and MS students are expected to be on-campus at UTK. Please reach out to discuss specific arrangements.
How long does it take to hear back after sending an inquiry?
PLACEHOLDER: We try to respond within 1–2 weeks. Due to the volume of inquiries, we may not be able to respond to all messages individually. If you haven't heard back in 2 weeks, a gentle follow-up is welcome.
