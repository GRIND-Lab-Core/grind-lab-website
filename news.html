GRIND Lab Receives NSF Grant for GeoAI-Powered Wildfire Risk Assessment

The GRIND Lab has been awarded a significant NSF grant to develop next-generation GeoAI models for wildfire risk assessment and community vulnerability mapping across the western United States. The project will integrate multi-source remote sensing, socioeconomic data, and physics-informed AI for near-real-time risk mapping.
