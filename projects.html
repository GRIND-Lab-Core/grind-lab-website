Our Research Projects

Advancing responsible geospatial AI to understand and mitigate coupled nature-human risks across scales.

Featured Project

GeoAI-Powered Wildfire Risk Assessment and Community Vulnerability Mapping

Developing explainable GeoAI models to assess wildfire risk and identify socially vulnerable communities in the western U.S. We integrate multi-source remote sensing, socioeconomic data, and physics-informed AI for near-real-time risk mapping.

2024 – 2027
NSF Funded
