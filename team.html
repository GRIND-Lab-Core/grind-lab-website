About

Dr. Bing Zhou is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography and Sustainability at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where he directs the GRIND Lab (Geospatial Responsible AI for Nature-human Dynamics Laboratory).

His work integrates remote sensing, human mobility, street-view imagery, social sensing, environmental observations, and other forms of geospatial data to better understand exposure, vulnerability, human behavior, and recovery across space and time.

Research Assistants

Biography of Xiaoshu Yang

Xiaoshu Yang is a Master's student in Computer Science at Rice University, where her research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, geospatial computing, and urban environmental analysis. Her current work focuses on multimodal learning and cross-modal interaction modeling for urban noise prediction, exploring how heterogeneous geospatial data and complex spatial relationships can be integrated to better understand and predict urban environmental dynamics. Her broader research interests include Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI), machine learning, and human–environment interactions, with a particular emphasis on understanding how geographic environments influence human perception, behavior, and everyday experiences. Through advanced computational models, she aims to uncover hidden spatial patterns and support more intelligent, sustainable, and human-centered urban environments.

Biography of Xuechun Yu

Xuechun Yu holds a Master of Urban Design from the University of Hong Kong. During three years as an urban planner, Yu worked on child-friendly city initiatives, including projects commissioned by UNICEF. That experience sparked an interest in how city design shapes everyday life and well-being. Yu’s research brings together human-centered GeoAI, multimodal urban sensing, and urban planning. Using urban imagery, soundscapes, and geospatial data, Yu studies how people experience cities and how those experiences can inform more equitable planning and design.

Biography of Shiyun Wen

Shiyun Wen is a Research Assistant with an interdisciplinary background in landscape architecture, software engineering, and artificial intelligence. Her research focuses on GeoAI, urban computing, and multimodal machine learning, with an emphasis on understanding and modeling complex urban environments. She develops data-driven and generative AI approaches that integrate geospatial, visual, and acoustic information to investigate urban perception, environmental conditions, and human–environment interactions. Her work aims to bridge computational methods and urban research to support more intelligent, sustainable, and human-centered cities.
